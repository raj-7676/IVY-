No internet after setupIvy downloads its speech model once. After that it only goes online if you press Check for updates.

One small model. No cloud.

Ivy runs a fine-tune of Qwen3-ASR-1.7B through llama.cpp on your own hardware. It hears and cleans up in a single pass, with no second AI rewriting your words.
